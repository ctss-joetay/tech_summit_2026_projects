A one-click 'Add Jayden's Snack' button was added to a nutrition tracking app to log a preset high-calorie, high-fat, low-protein/low-carb food item. The preset values (620 kcal, 45g fat, 4g protein, 2g carbs) are meant to represent an unhealthy snack like fried pork rinds. The button behaves like a manual entry, updating the nutrient bars and log automatically.
