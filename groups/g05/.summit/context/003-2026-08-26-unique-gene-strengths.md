The group reworked the genetics system so that every gene is mechanically unique rather than genes of the same category all having identical effects. Genes that share a mechanic, such as disease resistance, now each have their own distinct percentage strength (e.g., 10%, 9%, 4%), so no two genes behave identically even if they influence the same outcome. The dashboard/checkbox UI was updated to display each gene's specific value next to its name, though the exact files modified were not specified in this exchange.
