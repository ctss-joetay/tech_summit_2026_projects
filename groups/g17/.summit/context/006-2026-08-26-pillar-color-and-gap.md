Adjusted pillar (pipe) obstacle appearance and sizing in what appears to be a Flappy Bird-style game: pillars now have a green base body with a yellow rim marking the gap edges, and the gap between top and bottom pillars is fixed at 2 bird-diameters (56px) wide so the bird can reliably fit through, replacing the previous randomized gap of 3+ bird widths. Pillar widths remain randomized for visual variety.
