2D Sandbox

Left-click/drag on the world to place blocks, right-click/drag to erase. Select the Erase tool to left-click/drag erase too. Move the player with A/D or arrow keys, jump with Space. The yellow-highlighted cell in front of the player is your interaction target: press F to break it, G to place the selected block into it. Plant a Seed on grass near Water and wait a few seconds for it to grow into a tree.
